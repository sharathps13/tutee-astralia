      Bring the whole mob

      Australia is unusually family-friendly.

      A group of kangaroos is called a mob. Yours can come too: your partner and children under 18 are included in your own student visa application from the start.

      On your application: partner and children under 18 on your subclass 500.
      Partner work rights: 48 hours a fortnight, unlimited once you're in a master's or doctorate.
      We handle: dependant documents, extra funds evidence and school enrolment.
